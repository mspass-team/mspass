175 /* These are data at different stages of process. d_all is the
176 largest signal window that is assumed to have been initialized by the
177 load method for this object. d_decon is the
178 result of applying the preprocessor (signal processing) deconvolution to
179 all three components. d_decon is computed from d, which is a windowed
